STEP 1 — TOPIC GATE
============================================================

For each topic gate item:

1. Read the label meaning.

3. Read the meeting-specific not_match patterns.

direct:

inferential:
The content is related, but reader inference is needed; or the content
falls into a not_match pattern.

unrelated:
There is no relevant content.

Only direct counts as a label match.
