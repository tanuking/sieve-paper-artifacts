# Slot Boundaries

Pairwise boundary rules between slots. These boundaries are used for
rubric authoring and interpretation; the judge does not use them to
reclassify content across slots.

## T: Topic/Framing

- vs `O`: T captures substantive framing: subject, policy direction, and target. O captures the body's procedural disposition on an acted-on item. This slot when: Use T when the content explains what policy matter was being discussed, what substantive policy move was being pursued, and what target the move applied to. Other slot when: Use O when the content states what the body did to an item, such as approved, adopted, passed, rejected, referred, deferred, or recommended.
- vs `N`: T captures substantive framing: subject, policy direction, and target. N captures operative payload attached to an action. These are not mutually exclusive: the same summary clause can satisfy T as a policy direction or rule effect and also satisfy N as an operative payload when the rubric criteria for each slot support that use. This slot when: Use T when the content shows what policy move was being pursued for the subject and target, including a concrete rule effect that shows how the target would change. Other slot when: Use N when the content states what an action, ordinance, amendment, motion, or recommendation specifically required, limited, set, included, scoped, or conditioned.
- vs `B`: T captures what the discussion was about and what policy move was pursued. B captures agenda-setting background or precipitating context that shaped the current item or discussion. This slot when: Use T when the content identifies the substantive issue, policy direction, or target. Other slot when: Use B when the content states prior event, condition, process, problem, opposition, mediation, revision, failure, deadline, or other background that shaped the current item or its form.
- vs `S`: T captures substantive framing. S captures a recurring problem-side concern theme. This slot when: Use T when the content frames the policy subject, direction, and target. Other slot when: Use S when the content names what participants were concerned about in problem-side terms, especially a recurring or shared concern theme.

## O: Outcome/Position

- vs `T`: O captures the body's disposition on an acted-on item. T captures the substantive framing of the discussion. This slot when: Use O when the content states that a body approved, passed, adopted, rejected, deferred, referred, continued, authorized, directed, or recommended an item. Other slot when: Use T when the content states the policy subject, direction, and target rather than the body's action on an item.
- vs `N`: O is the action shell. N is the operative payload attached to the action. This slot when: Use O when the content states the disposition itself and the item acted on. Other slot when: Use N when the content states what the action required, limited, set, included, exempted, scoped, or conditioned.
- vs `U`: O captures what the body did in-session. U captures what remained pending or unresolved after the action or discussion. This slot when: Use O for the completed in-session disposition, such as a vote, recommendation, referral, approval, passage, or adoption. Other slot when: Use U for later handling, pending final action, future review, report-back, unresolved implementation, or an explicit return path.
- vs `B`: O captures what the body did. B captures the background context that shaped the item. This slot when: Use O when the content states the body's disposition. Other slot when: Use B when the content states the prompting condition for the item.

## B: Agenda-Setting Background / Precipitating Context

- vs `T`: B captures agenda-setting background or precipitating context. T captures the substantive policy framing. This slot when: Use B when the content states prior event, condition, process, problem, opposition, mediation, revision, failure, deadline, or other background that shaped the current item or its form. Other slot when: Use T when the content identifies the substantive policy subject, direction, or target.
- vs `N`: B captures background context that shaped the item. N captures the operative payload of the action. This slot when: Use B when the content states prior context, problem, deadline, barrier, mediation, revision, or other background that shaped the current item. Other slot when: Use N when the content states what the action itself required, changed, limited, or set.
- vs `L`: B captures background context for the item. L captures concrete concern, harm, obstacle, difficulty, loss, risk, or tradeoff content. This slot when: Use B when the content explains the prior context or formation path for the item, even if the background is a process defect or system-level issue. Other slot when: Use L when the content states a concrete concern, harm, obstacle, difficulty, loss, risk, or tradeoff.
- vs `S`: B captures agenda-setting background or precipitating context. S captures a recurring problem-side concern theme in the discussion. This slot when: Use B when the content states the prior context or condition that shaped the item or its form. Other slot when: Use S when the content names a concern theme discussed or synthesized across the discussion, even if that concern was not the formal background for the item.

## N: Outcome-Near Condition / Operative Payload

- vs `O`: N captures payload attached to an action. O captures the action shell. This slot when: Use N when the content states conditions, requirements, restrictions, permissions, scope, numbers, dates, deadlines, thresholds, or other operative content carried by the action. Other slot when: Use O when the content states that the body approved, passed, adopted, rejected, deferred, referred, recommended, or otherwise disposed of an item.
- vs `T`: N captures operative payload attached to an action. T captures substantive framing. These are not mutually exclusive when a concrete rule effect both shows policy direction and is carried by the action. This slot when: Use N when the content is attached to an action or acted-on item as a requirement, condition, scope, number, date, threshold, or other operative term. Other slot when: Use T when the same content functions as the policy direction or target of the discussion.
- vs `B`: N captures what the action does. B captures background context that shaped the item or discussion. This slot when: Use N when the content states the rule, condition, requirement, or restriction adopted, proposed, or carried by the action. Other slot when: Use B when the content states prior event, condition, process, problem, opposition, mediation, revision, failure, deadline, or other background that shaped the current item or its form.
- vs `U`: N captures operative payload. U captures later pending work or unresolved handling. This slot when: Use N for requirements, conditions, deadlines, report obligations, or review steps that are part of the action's payload. Other slot when: Use U when the content says that work, review, final action, implementation, or another item remains pending after the action or discussion.

## L: Concrete Concern

- vs `S`: L captures concrete concern content. S captures a recurring problem-side concern theme. This slot when: Use L when the content states a concrete concern, harm, obstacle, difficulty, loss, risk, or tradeoff. A local anchor is not required for Presence. Other slot when: Use S when the content is grounded as a recurring or shared concern theme across the discussion.
- vs `B`: L captures concrete concern content. B captures background context that shaped the item. This slot when: Use L when the summary states a concrete concern, harm, obstacle, difficulty, loss, risk, tradeoff, exposure, or inability. Other slot when: Use B when the summary states prior context, process, mediation, revision, deadline, or other background that shaped the item.
- vs `T`: L captures concrete concern content. T captures policy framing. This slot when: Use L when the content states a concrete problem-side concern, harm, obstacle, difficulty, loss, risk, or tradeoff. Other slot when: Use T when the content states the substantive policy subject, direction, and target.

## S: Shared Concern Synthesis

- vs `L`: S captures a recurring problem-side concern theme. L captures concrete concern content. This slot when: Use S when the content is grounded as a recurring or shared concern theme across the discussion. Other slot when: Use L when the content states a concrete concern, harm, obstacle, difficulty, loss, risk, or tradeoff. A local anchor is not required for Presence.
- vs `B`: S captures a concern theme. B captures the agenda-setting background or precipitating context. This slot when: Use S when the content names what participants were worried about, criticized, or treated as a recurring problem-side theme. Other slot when: Use B when the content states the background context that shaped the item or discussion.
- vs `T`: S captures concern-side content. T captures policy framing. This slot when: Use S when the content names the problem-side concern theme or tension. Other slot when: Use T when the content names the policy subject, direction, and target.
- vs `N`: S captures concerns about policy effects or tradeoffs. N captures operative payload. This slot when: Use S when the content states a concern, risk, tension, tradeoff, or criticized mechanism. Other slot when: Use N when the content states a requirement, condition, restriction, number, scope, or deadline carried by the action.

## U: Unresolved Tail / Future Return Path

- vs `O`: U captures unfinishedness after action or discussion. O captures the in-session disposition. This slot when: Use U when the content states that final action, review, report-back, implementation, drafting, further deliberation, or another workstream remains pending. Other slot when: Use O when the content states what the body did in the meeting.
- vs `N`: U captures pending handling. N captures operative payload, including future-facing requirements when they are part of the action. This slot when: Use U when the focus is that something remains unfinished or must return later. Other slot when: Use N when the future-facing item is a requirement, condition, deadline, or review step carried by the action.
- vs `B`: U captures what remains after action. B captures background context that shaped the item or discussion. This slot when: Use U for pending work, later handling, or unresolved tail after the action or discussion. Other slot when: Use B for prior event, condition, process, problem, opposition, mediation, revision, failure, deadline, or other background that shaped the current item or its form.
